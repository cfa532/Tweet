dTweet Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

1. Introduction

dTweet, operated by cfa532, provides the dTweet mobile application and related services (collectively, the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you. It applies to the dTweet app for iPhone and iPad.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use dTweet, we may collect the following information:

  • Account and profile information: your username, authentication credentials, display name, optional email address, profile description, avatar, user identifier, and account settings.
  • User content and activity: posts, comments, replies, direct messages, uploaded photos, videos or audio, reports, follows, reactions, bookmarks, and related timestamps and identifiers. Content you choose to publish may be visible to other users according to the audience and privacy settings you select.
  • Device and technical information: information needed to connect to and operate the Service, such as IP address, app and operating-system version, request timestamps, network information, and error or diagnostic information.
  • Support and safety communications: information you provide when contacting us, reporting content, or requesting help.

3. Device Permissions and Media

dTweet may request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, and notifications. These permissions are used only for the feature you choose—for example, capturing or selecting media for a post, reply, profile image, or message, or receiving notifications. Media is transmitted to the Service only when you choose to upload or send it. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

The iOS app does not use advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It does not use browser cookies for app activity.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate accounts;
  • provide posts, profiles, messaging, media, notifications, and other interactive features;
  • store preferences and maintain the Service;
  • respond to support requests and communications;
  • detect, investigate, and address abuse, security incidents, policy violations, and technical problems;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies; and
  • understand performance and improve reliability and functionality.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share information only as follows:

  • With other users: profile information, public posts, and interactions are shared according to the features you use and the privacy settings you select. Direct messages are shared with their intended participants.
  • With service providers: infrastructure and technical providers may process information only to operate, secure, and support the Service. They are required to protect information consistently with this policy and applicable law.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse, or defend rights and the security of the Service.
  • In a business transfer: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account information and user content while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information and whether it is needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or enforcement of our agreements.

You can request deletion directly in the app by opening Settings → Delete Account. After a deletion request is completed, the app clears its local account caches and the Service deletes or de-identifies account information and associated content, except information that must be retained for a limited period for legal, security, backup, or abuse-prevention purposes. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by emailing cfa532@yahoo.com.

7. Your Choices

  • Edit your profile and content using the controls available in the app.
  • Choose whether a post is public or private where that option is available.
  • Change camera, microphone, photo-library, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app or contact us about your privacy rights.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No Internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public posts or other content intended for broad distribution.

9. Children’s Privacy

dTweet is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Service, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may display content or links from third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Service, legal requirements, or our practices. We will post the updated policy at this address and revise the effective date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate means.
